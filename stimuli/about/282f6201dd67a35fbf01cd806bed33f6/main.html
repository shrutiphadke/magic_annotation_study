Tuesday, May 12th, 2026

The Moon continues its transit of Pisces until 8:05 PM EDT, when it heads into Aries, and we instinctively seek a fresh start. Venus forms a quintile with Saturn today, and we’re inclined to maneuver and plan tastefully. We may be showcasing our reliability and loyalty.

As the day advances, we approach a Mercury-Jupiter sextile that's exact very early tomorrow and is strongly influencing today. This transit is gracious. It stirs optimism. We're alert and resourceful, seeking the positive side of situations and solutions to problems. We take a more philosophical approach to thinking and are more open to learning. It's a time of optimism, new insights, and cooperation. Our minds open, and we seek a positive focal point. This is a time when we more readily exercise good judgment. Today may be a good time to reach a mutually beneficial agreement or arrangement.

The Moon is void from 6:05 AM EDT, with the Moon's last aspect before changing signs (a square to Venus), until the Moon enters Aries at 8:05 PM EDT.
