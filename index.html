Research Study

Website & Product Evaluation

Thank you for participating. In this study you will view 5 pairs of web pages. Each pair shows one website's About page alongside one of its products.

For each pair, you will answer 10 short questions about the product and what the website claims. Most questions are dropdowns; a couple ask for short typed answers.

The study takes approximately 15–20 minutes. Your responses are anonymous and identified only by your Prolific ID.

Before you begin

  • Use a desktop or laptop computer (mobile is not supported).
  • You can scroll inside each page to read the full content.
  • Please read both pages carefully before answering.
